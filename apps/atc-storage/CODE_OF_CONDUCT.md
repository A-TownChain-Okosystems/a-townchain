# Code of Conduct — atc-storage

## Respectful Collaboration

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors within the A-TownChain ecosystem.

### Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

### Reporting Issues

Enforcement and reporting are handled according to official A-TownChain governance channels.
